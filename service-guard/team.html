Adam Behringer

Co-Founder

Adam grew up in Indiana and now resides in Nashville, TN. He spent his childhood on a farm, around small businesses, and working in his dad's shop, learning to refinish and build furniture.

He began his career advising healthcare business owners through the sale of their companies. More recently, Adam has been an investor at Shore Capital, a leading lower middle market private equity firm, where he focused on investing in small home and B2B services businesses (residential and commercial roofing, generator repair, fiber internet, chimney repair, well drilling, smart home technology integration), software, healthcare, and more.
