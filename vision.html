Who We Are

Built by Entrepreneurs,
Not a Typical Insurance Company

Service Guard was founded with a clear conviction: roofing contractors deserve better tools to protect their work, grow their business, and serve their customers.

Mission

Transforming Workmanship Liability Into Strategic Advantage

To help roofing businesses enhance close rates, reduce risk, strengthen customer trust, and improve financial outcomes by transforming workmanship liability into strategic advantage.

Vision

The Leading Risk and Warranty Platform for Roofing Businesses

To become the leading risk and warranty platform for roofing businesses — where all roofing contractors have access to better protection, aligned incentives, and the ability to turn warranties from a cost center into a profit driver.
