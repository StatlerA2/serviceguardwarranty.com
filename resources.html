What's Covered vs. What's Not

Understanding the boundaries of the workmanship warranty.

Covered

  • Installation errors (improper nailing, flashing, underlayment, sealant)
  • Defects resulting in leaks or water intrusion from workmanship
  • Premature roof failure attributable to how it was installed
  • Wind and algae warranty coverage

Not Covered

  • Material or manufacturer defects (covered by manufacturer warranty)
  • Storm, wind, hail, or weather-related damage (covered by homeowner's insurance)
  • Normal wear and tear, aging, or cosmetic issues
  • Damage from third-party modifications after installation
Eligibility

Contractor Qualification Criteria

Service Guard underwrites all roofing partners. To qualify for the program, contractors should meet the following criteria.

Minimum Requirements

  • Valid state roofing contractor license
  • 3+ years in business
  • Minimum 100 roofs completed per year
  • Fully insured (General Liability / Umbrella)
  • Manufacturer certification preferred (GAF, Owens Corning, IKO, etc.)

What Disqualifies a Contractor

  • Storm chasing operations (more than 30% restoration revenue)
  • Claim history exceeding 15% of roofs completed over the last 3 years
  • Loss ratio above 40% triggers program cancellation
  • No valid license or insufficient insurance coverage
