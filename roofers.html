Why Contractors Choose Service Guard

Win More Jobs Without Spending More on Marketing

When homeowners compare bids, trust is the deciding factor. An insurance-backed warranty signals that your workmanship meets the standards of a regulated insurance carrier, giving customers the confidence to choose you before you ever pick up a tool.

Polls and surveys report that customers would pay more for an insurance-backed warranty

Protect Your Bottom Line From Workmanship Callbacks

Every roofing contractor knows the cost of a callback — truck rolls, materials, labor, and the hit to your reputation. When a workmanship issue surfaces two or three years down the road, it comes straight out of your pocket.

With a Service Guard warranty, an admitted insurance carrier pays covered claims — not you. You transfer long-tail workmanship liability off your balance sheet, eliminating open-ended warranty exposure that can erode retained earnings or complicate a future sale of your business.

Claims are paid by the insurance carrier, not out of your pocket

Give Homeowners a Reason to Trust You on the Spot

For most homeowners, a new roof is one of the biggest and most emotional home investments they'll ever make. They're comparing bids, reading reviews, and looking for reassurance that their money is well spent.

When you hand them an insurance-backed warranty, the conversation changes. It's no longer just your word, it's a legally binding commitment backed by a regulated carrier. That level of confidence accelerates decisions and sets you apart as the preferred contractor of choice.

A transferable, insurance-backed warranty makes your proposal the easy choice
